Immersive Reading

Customize your experience with beautiful page textures like Parchment, Old Paper, or a high-contrast Night Mode. Perfect fit-to-width scaling for landscape mode.

Advanced Annotations

A robust canvas engine lets you draw, highlight, and place draggable sticky notes anywhere on the page, complete with full Undo/Redo capability.

⭕

Smart Machine Learning

Using Google ML Kit, simply draw a circle around any text in the PDF to instantly extract it via OCR for copying or translating.

📱

Tilt-to-Turn

Put your thumbs away. Navigate through your books simply by tilting your device left or right, powered by precise accelerometer sensor tracking.

🎧

Text-to-Speech

Rest your eyes and let the app read to you. The built-in TTS engine seamlessly extracts text from the page and reads it aloud.

🌧️

Focus Mode

Block out distractions with built-in, lifecycle-safe ambient rain sounds. Easily control the volume from the advanced settings menu.
